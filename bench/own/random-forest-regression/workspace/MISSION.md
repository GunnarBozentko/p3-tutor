# Mission: Random Forest Regression

## Why

The learner want st olearn this topic so they can apply random forest regression to large datasets. They also want to know the mathematical background behind the model, as well as the bias-variance tradeoff. 

## Success looks like

Accurately applying the model and concepts behind random forest regression, and performing well on an exam on these items. 

## Constraints

The student has two weeks to learn and prepare before their exam.

## Out of scope

Do not learn about separate ensemble models besides random forest regression right now.
