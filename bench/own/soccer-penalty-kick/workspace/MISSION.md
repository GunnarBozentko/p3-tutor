# Mission: Improving Penalty Kicks

## Why

The learner wants to learn this topic so they can be more confidently taking penalty kicks for their team. He is a forward and improving penalty kicks is an important part to improve his game. 

## Success looks like

Consistently placing the ball in the corner of the goal with power past the keeper. Scoring the goal is the end game in this case.

## Constraints

The student has limited time after school with daylight to practice their penalty kicks. 

## Out of scope

Do not learn other parts of soccer besides penalty kicks.
