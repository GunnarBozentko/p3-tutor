Two formulas

Height at the goal line, no drag or spin: t = d / (v cos a), h = h0 + d tan a - 0.5 x 9.81 x t². With d = 11 m, v = 25 m/s, h0 = 0.11 m: 12 degrees gives 1.46 m, 17 degrees gives 2.43 m, 18 degrees gives 2.63 m. The bar is 2.44 m.

Lateral miss: m = 11 x tan(e). A 1 degree error gives 0.19 m, and a 3 degree error gives 0.58 m.
