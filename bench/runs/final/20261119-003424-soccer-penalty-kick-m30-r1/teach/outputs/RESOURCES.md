# Penalty Kick Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "Coaches – Don't Make This Mistake When Teaching Kicking Technique" (Soccer Fitness, Zafar, 2017)](https://soccerfitness.ca/?p=6087)
  Plant foot beside the ball, contact below centre lifts the ball, and why lean is not the whole story. Use for: plant foot and height.
- [Article: "Plant Foot, Body Lean, and Accuracy in the Instep Drive" (Coaching American Soccer)](https://coachingamericansoccer.com/?p=9457)
  Plant foot distance of 6 to 8 inches, toes at the target, slight forward lean. Use for: plant foot and lean.
- [Article: "Striking the Ball" (CoachDeck, Brownlee, 2018)](https://coachdeck.com/blogs/news/striking-the-ball)
  Strike the centre, knee snap, lean as a side effect of foot geometry. Use for: contact point and power.
- [Article: "Coaches – Don't Make This Technical Mistake With Your Players" (Soccer Fitness, 2016)](https://www.soccerfitness.ca/?p=5536)
  Height set by the contact point; body over the ball is irrelevant to height. Use for: contact point and lean.
- [Research record: "Modeling the two-dimensional accuracy of soccer kicks"](https://lida.sport-iat.de/twm/Record/4047769?lng=en)
  Abstract of a study of 20 players and 8,466 kicks: side-foot more accurate than instep, error variance grows with speed. Use for: the speed and accuracy trade-off.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local: your own team's goalkeeper and coach.
  Use for: filming your plant foot and testing placement against a real keeper.

## Gaps

- No peer-reviewed source read in full on penalty-specific technique (plant foot, lean and launch angle). The PMC and MDPI papers found in search were blocked from automated reading.
- No measured link between foot path angle and ball launch angle.
