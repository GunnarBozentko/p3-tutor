# Plant foot belief: behind the ball gives more power

On quiz 0001 the learner chose to plant the foot well behind the ball for a longer swing and more power, which is the open misconception M2. They also merged striking surface and contact point into one step, and gave the penalty mark distance in yards.

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s1, items q3 and q4.
**Implications:** Teach how plant position sets body lean and ball height before any work on power.
