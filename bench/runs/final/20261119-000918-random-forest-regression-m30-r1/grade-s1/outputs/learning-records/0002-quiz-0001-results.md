# Tree mechanics solid; extrapolation gap persists; variance link missing

On quiz 0001 the learner chose the squared-error split and predicted a leaf mean correctly. They explained overfitting only as memorization, without linking it to variance. They again expected a tree to continue a trend beyond the training range (the extrapolation misconception, M2).

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s1, items q1 to q4.
