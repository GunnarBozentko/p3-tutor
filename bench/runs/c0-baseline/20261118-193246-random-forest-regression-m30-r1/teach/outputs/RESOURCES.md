# Random Forest Regression Resources

## Knowledge

- [scikit-learn user guide: Ensembles (bagging, random forests)](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/ensemble.html)
  Official documentation for the model being applied. Use for: bagging definition, RandomForestRegressor parameters such as max_features, applying the model to data.
- [Random forest (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_forest)
  Overview of the algorithm, averaging for regression, bagging versus feature randomness. Use for: a quick statement of how predictions are made.
- [When do Random Forests work? (arXiv 2504.12860)](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12860v1)
  Recent analysis of variance reduction and its limits in forests. Use for: going beyond the variance-and-correlation account, later.
- [EPFL BIO-322 notes on ensembles](https://bio322.epfl.ch/weeks/10/ensembles.html)
  University course page on ensembles. Use for: a second explanation of why averaging helps.
- Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman, The Elements of Statistical Learning, ch. 15 (cited by name, URL not yet checked)
  Source of the variance-of-an-average formula with correlation. Use for: the mathematical background.
