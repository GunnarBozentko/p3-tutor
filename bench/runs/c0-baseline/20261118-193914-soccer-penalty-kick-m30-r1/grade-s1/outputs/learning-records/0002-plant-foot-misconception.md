# Plant foot placement: misconception M2 still open

On quiz 0001 (submission s1, item q4) the learner chose to plant the foot well behind the ball for a longer swing and more power. This is M2, and it matches the habit already in NOTES.md of lifting shots over the bar.
They did correctly identify the follow-through and the 11 m (12 yard) penalty distance, but merged the striking surface and contact point into "kick the ball" when listing the parts.

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s1, items q2, q3, q4.
**Implications:** The next lesson should address why the plant foot goes beside the ball, and why the striking surface and contact point change direction and power.
