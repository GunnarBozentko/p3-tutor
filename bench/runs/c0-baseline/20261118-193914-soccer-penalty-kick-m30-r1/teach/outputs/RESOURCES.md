# Penalty Kick Resources

## Knowledge

- [Review: "Biomechanical Characteristics and Determinants of Instep Soccer Kick", Kellis & Katis, J Sports Sci Med 2007](https://jssm.org/jssm-06-154.xml-abst)
  Standard review of instep kick research. Use for: ball speed vs accuracy, contact point and spin, and the point that there is no consensus on plant foot placement.
- [Journal of Sports Science and Medicine, penalty kick study](https://jssm.org/08-2-230.p_d_f)
  Study of penalty kicks. Use for: instep kick in penalties and the speed/accuracy trade-off.
- [Clagg 2009, kinetic analyses of maximal effort soccer kicks in female collegiate athletes](https://people.stfx.ca/smackenz/Courses/HK474/Midterm/Clagg%202009%20Kinetic%20analyses%20of%20maximal%20effort%20soccer%20kicks%20in%20female%20collegiate%20athletes.pdf)
  Course-hosted copy of a kinetics study. Use for: trunk lean and plant-leg angle in collegiate players.
- [Kick (association football), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_(association_football))
  General overview, secondary source. Use for: a quick orientation only, not as proof.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local: a coach or teammate who films your penalties from the side.
  Use for: checking plant foot distance and chest position at contact.

## Gaps

- No peer-reviewed study found that isolates trunk lean or plant foot distance and penalty kick height. The lean-to-height link is coaching consensus plus physical reasoning.
