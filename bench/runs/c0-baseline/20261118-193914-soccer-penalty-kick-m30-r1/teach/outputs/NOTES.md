# Notes

The learner knows the basics of the penalty kick. They know the approach, where to plant the foot, and which part of the foot to kick the ball. The usually hit the target of the goal, but it gets saved or misses wide occasionally.

They do not know why each part of the technique changes where the ball goes and with how much power. They do not know why striking with the laces gives more power and less accuracy, and vice versa with the inside of the foot. They think power matters more than placement, so they tend to kick the ball as hard as possible. The issue is that they plant their foot too far behind the ball to get a longer swing. This causes the ball to lift and can send the ball over the bar.
